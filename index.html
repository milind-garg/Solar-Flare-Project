Predictive Pipeline Architecture

From Satellite Telemetry to Space Weather Warning

A four-stage pipeline processes raw L1 observation streams through temporal feature tensors into continuous flare nowcasts and 30-minute advance probability forecasts.

STAGE 01 · OBSERVATIONS
Telemetry Acquisition
1 Hz raw counts resampled to a uniform 1-minute cadence for SoLEXS SDD1/SDD2 and HEL1OS CZT channels.
STAGE 02 · TENSOR FEATURES
Sliding 8-Feature Window
60-minute window extracting SXR log flux, HXR counts, 1m & 5m derivatives, spectral hardness, normalized excess, and QPP FFT power.
STAGE 03 · NEURAL INFERENCE
CNN-LSTM Forecaster
1D convolutions capture fast impulsive transients; stacked LSTM tracks cumulative thermal growth to output P(flare in 30m).
STAGE 04 · OPERATIONS
Threshold Alerts
Calibrated alert dispatch against configurable decision threshold τ (nominal 0.35) with NOAA R-scale severity levels.
Solar Physics Foundation

The Neupert Effect in Flare Dynamics

In solar flare physics, the hard X-ray impulsive burst typically peaks before the soft X-ray thermal peak. As non-thermal electrons collide with the dense chromosphere, chromospheric evaporation fills coronal loops with hot plasma, accumulating thermal soft X-ray emission:

FSXR(t) ∝ ∫t₀t FHXR(t') dt'  ⟹  dFSXR/dt ∝ FHXR(t)

By tracking HEL1OS impulsive bursts during the SXR rise phase, the pipeline identifies the acceleration peak with a median Neupert lag of +134 seconds, providing advance warning of the thermal maximum.

01
Pre-Flare Energy Storage
Magnetic shear accumulates in coronal loops. Quasi-periodic pulsations appear in pre-flare background.
02
Impulsive Phase (HEL1OS Peak)
Magnetic reconnection accelerates electrons downward. Hard X-ray bremsstrahlung spikes (10–160 keV).
03
Thermal Gradual Peak (SoLEXS Peak)
Evaporated plasma fills coronal loops. Soft X-rays peak 2–15 minutes later, dictating GOES flare class.
